## Common questions

**The app crashed or got stuck. What should I do?**
Try fully closing the app and reopening it. If the problem keeps happening, email me with the steps you took before it crashed, and I'll look into it.

**Can I suggest new questions?**
Yes, please. New question ideas, requests for new categories, or general feedback are all welcome. Send them to the email above.

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes. The app runs entirely on your device and does not need an internet connection to play.

**Is my data being collected?**
No. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html) for details.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by me. You can request one through [Apple's refund page](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
